Skate Helsinki

Kallio → Hermanni → Suvilahti → Kalasatama → Kruunusillat

Every street, path, bridge and building between Vallila, Kallio, Hakaniemi, Sörnäinen, Hermanni, Kalasatama and Korkeasaari, built from OpenStreetMap. Skate your spots, roll over Merihaansilta, Finkensilta and Kruunuvuorensilta, and session the Suvilahti DIY.
